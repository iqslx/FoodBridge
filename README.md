# FoodBridge

FoodBridge connects people and businesses with surplus food to charities that can collect it and share it with their communities.

## For donors

- Create an account and sign in immediately.
- Post food donations with a description, quantity, expiry date and pickup address.
- Edit or remove active donations.
- Review requests from charities and accept or reject them.
- Track donations and confirm when food has been collected.
- View the history of collected and expired donations.

## For charities

- Register an organization with its license number, city and address, then sign in immediately.
- Browse available food donations.
- Search by food description and filter by minimum quantity and pickup area.
- View donation details and send a collection request.
- Follow each request from submission to acceptance or rejection, and collection when accepted.

## For administrators

- Sign in through a dedicated admin portal.
- Review donor and charity accounts, including organization details.
- Deactivate or reactivate accounts.
- Monitor donations and recent platform activity.

## Across the platform

- In-app notifications for requests, decisions, donation changes and collection updates.
- Separate workspaces with clear donor, charity and admin roles.
- Responsive pages for desktop and mobile screens.
- Expiry dates interpreted through the end of the selected day in Saudi Arabia time.
- Expired and collected donations are removed from the available donations list.
